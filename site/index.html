Hearthworks

A cosy town builder where the villagers plan the town.

Every log, plank and loaf is carried by a villager until you invent machines to do it. They notice shortages, decide what to build and where, and grow from a hamlet into a civilisation: boats between islands, seasons, trade, and ideas that travel next door with visitors.

  • TypeScript
  • Deterministic simulation
  • Self-planning villagers
  • Design docs as game data

The Commute

Stockport to Manchester Piccadilly, one purchase at a time.

A public transport tycoon on a 3D map of the real line. Run Stockport station, earn the fares, buy the next station up the line, then the line itself, the buses and the trams, and watch every purchase appear on the map while the day turns overhead. Locked stations wait in the fog with their price.

  • Three.js
  • TypeScript
  • Seeded simulation
  • Balanced by a bot

Door Duty

Every client is a puzzle with a rush hour.

Run a firm fitting entrances: revolving doors, sliding doors, and later the ticket gates at the station. Design a frontage, test it against a 40-second rush of simulated people, and sign it off if the client is happy. Fitted clients pay maintenance; spend it on research and the town opens up. Inside jobs teach crowd paradoxes, where the obvious fix is the wrong one.

  • Crowd physics
  • One HTML page
  • No dependencies
  • Works offline

Final Call

Start with one gate and one small plane.

An airport management game in the browser: check-in, security, gates, baggage, the airfield and the board. Grow from a grass airfield into the Airport of the Year. Managers and tips for players who'd rather not sweat the details; full control for those who do. It runs on phones, tablets and desktops, and a long headless simulation keeps it balanced.

  • Canvas
  • One HTML page
  • Headless simulation
  • Phone to desktop
